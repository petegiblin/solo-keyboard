Solo Keyboard

Solo is a keyboard you can move: keys on the left or the right at the size that suits your thumb, split for two thumbs on wide screens, and a writing pad beside the keys. It works offline in seven languages and never asks for Full Access.

Turn it on

Open Settings, then General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, and choose Solo. In any app, hold the globe key and pick Solo.

Move the keys

Touch and hold the 123 key, or the bar above the keys, for the menu: side, size, language and more. On wide screens the Side row also offers Split.

Help and feedback

Found a problem or have a question? Report a problem. You need a free GitHub account to post. Please do not include anything private.

Privacy

Solo collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
